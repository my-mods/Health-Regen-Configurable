Initial release of Health Regen - Configurable.
Combine human and vampire regeneration in one configurable mod.
Add separate 0–5% regeneration rates, with 0.25% steps through 2% and 0.5% steps above 2%.
Stop adding healing at full recoverable health or the current vampire segment ceiling.
Add Combat regeneration and Restore vampire segments toggles, both Off by default.
Apply settings through the shared save-load flow without background settings polling.
